Question 1. Do you have to do anything else to ensure that this I/O privilege setting is saved and
restored properly when you subsequently switch from one environment to another? Why?

No because the IO privilege level is stored in the IOPL bits of EFLAGS and JOS saves and restores
an environment's full trapframe during switches. The EFLAGS is part of struct Trapframe and thus the
IOPL is saved and restored automatically by iret when the environment runs again.
